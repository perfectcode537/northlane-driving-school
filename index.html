Questions

Good to know.

I have never driven before. Is that OK?

That is how most of our students start. Your first lesson happens in a quiet car park and you only move on when you feel ready.

Do you pick me up?

Yes. We pick you up and drop you off at home, school or work anywhere in our area, at no extra cost.

Manual or automatic?

Both. Automatic is usually quicker to learn; manual gives you a licence for both kinds of car.

Can I change or cancel a lesson?

Move or cancel any lesson up to 24 hours before, free, from your phone.

How many lessons will I need?

Most beginners need 20 to 30 hours. After your first lesson your instructor will give you an honest estimate.
